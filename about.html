About Us

Our Story

Nearly two decades of trusted service in Papua

PT. Pengembangan Jaya Papua (PJP) is a reputable company that has been providing exceptional services since its establishment in 2005. With a strong focus on labor supply, PJP caters to various industries locally and nationally.

Our commitment to excellence is evident in our track record of delivering high-quality solutions and satisfying our clients' needs. Over the years, we have expanded our operations and now employ a skilled workforce of 967 individuals across the Lowland and Highland areas.

At PJP, we prioritize creating job opportunities for the younger generation and promoting diversity in our workforce. With 202 Papuan and 765 non-Papuan employees, we embrace inclusivity and equal opportunity.

We pride ourselves on our professionalism and dedication to providing utmost satisfaction to our clients. Through our labor supply services, we contribute to industry growth and success while ensuring the well-being and contentment of both our users and employees.

To achieve our goals, we have implemented the Strengthen Industrial Relationship (IR) Program, which includes a comprehensive employee "Work Performances" control system. This streamlines administrative processes and ensures prompt resolution of employment matters in compliance with company regulations and labor law.

At PJP, we strive to maintain stability, uphold integrity, and remain competitive in the industry. Our reliable and skilled workers are committed to delivering outstanding performance, further enhancing our reputation as a trusted partner.
