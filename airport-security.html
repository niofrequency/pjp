Mozes Kilangin Airport — Food Security & Hygiene

📅 October 5, 2023🏷️ Food Safety
Ensuring Excellence In The Skies

PT. Pengembangan Jaya Papua's In-Flight Catering Food Passes the Test

The aviation industry is a world of precision and excellence, where every aspect of the passenger experience is carefully considered. Among the many factors contributing to a memorable journey, in-flight catering holds a special place. PT. Pengembangan Jaya Papua has earned a stellar reputation not only for its operations but also for its exceptional in-flight catering food, recently passing a rigorous examination by the Mozes Kilangin Airport Health Department that ensured the highest standards of food security and hygiene.

PT. Pengembangan Jaya Papua understands that dining in the sky is a unique experience that demands culinary excellence. Its commitment to delivering exquisite meals begins with a dedication to using premium ingredients, handled with care and expertise. The team of skilled chefs is passionate about crafting dishes that not only meet but exceed passengers' expectations.

In recognition of the diverse tastes and dietary preferences of travelers, PT. Pengembangan Jaya Papua offers a versatile menu that caters to a wide range of culinary desires. Whether a passenger is vegetarian, an adventurer in gastronomy, or has specific dietary needs, PJP has tailored options to meet those preferences.

Food safety is paramount in in-flight catering, and PT. Pengembangan Jaya Papua takes this responsibility very seriously. The team adheres to strict hygiene and food safety protocols, ensuring that passengers enjoy not only delicious meals but also meals that are safe for consumption. Its kitchen facilities are equipped with the latest technology and maintained to the highest cleanliness standards.

Recently, PT. Pengembangan Jaya Papua's commitment to food safety and hygiene faced a comprehensive examination by the Mozes Kilangin Airport Health Department. This examination involved a battery of tests and checks, including assessments of the chemicals used in food preparation.

One critical aspect of the examination was the testing of various chemicals used in the preparation of in-flight catering food. The Health Department conducted thorough checks, including assessments such as the Methanyl Yellow test and the Rhodamine test, aimed at confirming that the food is free from harmful substances and meets stringent regulatory standards.

The outcome of the examination was a resounding success for PT. Pengembangan Jaya Papua. Its in-flight catering food passed with flying colors, reaffirming the company's commitment to delivering not only delicious but also safe meals to passengers.
