Freeport's Visit

📅 June 1, 2023🏷️ Client Relations

During a routine inspection, PT. Freeport Indonesia carried out a thorough assessment of PT. Pengembangan Jaya Papua's operations, reviewing the company's food safety measures and evaluating the quality of the catering staff working on site.

PJP's commitment to upholding the highest standards of food safety was clear throughout the inspection. The team demonstrated strict hygiene practices, ensuring that every dish prepared and served met the best standards of quality and safety.

The inspection also highlighted the skill of PJP's experienced chefs and dedicated staff. Their careful buffet preparation using fresh ingredients showed a clear commitment to excellence, giving corporate clients such as PT. Freeport Indonesia confidence in the diverse, high-quality menus on offer.

Throughout the inspection process, the professionalism and competence of PJP's staff stood out. Their expertise in handling food and maintaining cleanliness across catering operations left a positive and lasting impression on the PT. Freeport Indonesia inspection team.

As a trusted partner, PJP serves local companies across Timika's industrial sector, with a particular focus on mining, construction, and heavy-equipment operations. This specialization allows the company to concentrate on delivering high-quality food that supports the wellbeing and productivity of the workforce it serves.

The success of this inspection by PT. Freeport Indonesia reaffirmed confidence in PJP's ability to consistently deliver delicious, nutritious meals while upholding the highest standards of food safety and quality. This steadfast commitment to excellence continues to set PJP apart in the catering industry, strengthening its position as a leading catering service provider in Timika, Papua, Indonesia.
