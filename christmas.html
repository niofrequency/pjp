Christmas Celebration

📅 December 16, 2023🏷️ Culture

During the peaceful moments of Christmas, PT. Pengembangan Jaya Papua organized a special gathering for its staff at a church. This wasn't just any event; the church atmosphere was enriched as staff members were encouraged to bring their children along.

One of the most anticipated moments was the opportunity for the children to take pictures with Santa Claus, a figure synonymous with the joy of Christmas. The cheerful laughter of the children echoed throughout the church, adding to the festive spirit.

Not stopping there, PT. Pengembangan Jaya Papua showcased its care by presenting gifts to the families of its staff members. These weren't just gifts; they symbolized appreciation and recognition for the dedication and hard work shown throughout the year.

With a Christmas atmosphere filled with love and warmth, this gathering not only strengthened the bond among the PT. Pengembangan Jaya Papua staff but also emphasized that the spirit of Christmas is about sharing, appreciating, and creating lasting memories with loved ones.
