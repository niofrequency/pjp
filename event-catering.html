Event Catering

Celebrations & Occasions

Every Event, Catered With Care

PT. Pengembangan Jaya Papua, a renowned industrial catering provider, has also built deep expertise in event catering. With extensive experience and a commitment to excellence, we have become a trusted partner for special events across Timika, Papua.

Through our buffet-style catering services, we cater to weddings, church events, religious festivals, and school activities — ensuring that every event is memorable and enjoyable.

For weddings, we understand the importance of the day and strive to create a culinary experience that complements the joyous atmosphere. Our skilled chefs and dedicated staff work with the couple to craft a customised menu based on their preferences and cultural traditions, from appetisers to desserts.

Church events and religious festivals are moments of reverence and celebration. We offer menu options that cater to different dietary requirements and religious preferences, with close attention to detail so every event delivers both spiritual fulfilment and gastronomic satisfaction.

School activities call for catering that accommodates diverse tastes and nutritional needs. We provide balanced, nutritious food that energises students, offering menu choices that consider dietary restrictions and allergies while staying visually appealing and delicious.

Across all our event catering, we maintain high standards of food safety, quality, and presentation. Our professional, courteous staff handle every detail, ensuring a seamless catering experience from planning through to clean-up.

  • Weddings, church events, religious festivals and school activities
  • Customised menus built around cultural traditions and preferences
  • Dietary, allergy and religious requirements accommodated
  • Full-service delivery — from planning to clean-up
  • Professional, courteous on-site catering staff
  • Consistent food safety, quality and presentation standards
