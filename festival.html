Independence Day

📅 August 19, 2023🏷️ Culture

PT. Pengembangan Jaya Papua joined in celebrating Indonesia’s Independence Day as part of the "We Are One" Festival Budaya, hosted by PT. Freeport Indonesia. The event featured a dance performance and a car decoration contest, both built around the theme of "Sulawesi Utara."

PJP's team took part in both the vibrant dance performance and the creative car decoration contest, paying tribute to the rich cultural heritage of the Sulawesi Utara tribe.

The team's dance performance secured 3rd place in the competition, with dedication and teamwork shining through on stage in a display of unity and culture.

The team's car, decorated to reflect the traditions of the Sulawesi Utara tribe, placed 5th in the car decoration contest — its vibrant colors and detailed design capturing the spirit of the tribal heritage it represented.

The festival gave PJP's team an opportunity to celebrate Indonesia's Independence Day through culture, creativity, and unity, and the company extended its thanks to everyone who supported the team throughout the event.

The results stand as a reminder of what a shared sense of purpose and pride can achieve — and of the diversity that makes the "We Are One" spirit meaningful.
