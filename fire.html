Fire Safety Training

📅 January 19, 2024🏷️ Safety

PT. Pengembangan Jaya Papua recently conducted a comprehensive Fire Safety Training, focusing on equipping participants with essential knowledge and skills to respond effectively in case of a fire emergency. The training covered crucial aspects such as the proper use of fire blankets, understanding fire exits in buildings, and mastering the operation of fire extinguishers.

Fire Blanket Techniques

Participants were educated on the correct utilization of fire blankets to swiftly and safely extinguish small fires. Emphasizing hands-on experience, the training equipped individuals with the skills needed to handle fire incidents confidently.

Navigating Fire Exits

Understanding the layout of fire exits within buildings is paramount for a quick and orderly evacuation during emergencies. The training provided insights into identifying and safely utilizing fire exits, emphasizing the importance of clear pathways for evacuation.

Effective Fire Extinguisher Usage

A significant part of the training was dedicated to the proper handling of fire extinguishers. Participants learned about the various types of extinguishers and their specific uses. Practical demonstrations ensured that everyone could confidently operate a fire extinguisher, enhancing their ability to respond promptly to fire incidents.

This Fire Safety Training not only enhanced participants' awareness of fire prevention and response but also instilled a sense of responsibility for maintaining a safe environment. PT. Pengembangan Jaya Papua remains committed to fostering a culture of safety and preparedness among its team members, contributing to a secure and resilient workplace.
