JPP New Training Center

📅 September 20, 2023🏷️ Facilities

In the heart of Papua, an exciting new chapter in education and professional development is underway. PT. Jasa Prima Papua (JPP), a pioneering institution committed to excellence, marked the inception of its new training center with a groundbreaking ceremony that stands as a symbol of progress and opportunity for the region.

The new training center reflects JPP's dedication to empowering individuals through education and skills development. The project is designed to create a hub of knowledge and expertise, opening doors to a brighter future for aspiring professionals across Papua.

The groundbreaking ceremony itself is a tradition dating back centuries, symbolizing the start of a new venture and the shared aspirations of the community it will serve. It took place on a sunny morning, on 20 September 2023, in the presence of esteemed guests, community leaders, and JPP’s leadership team.

The highlight of the ceremony was the placement of the foundation stone by Paulus Dumais, Assistant I for Government and Social Affairs, symbolizing the strong and enduring foundation on which the training center will be built. The stone-laying was accompanied by words of hope, inspiration, and commitment from the leaders and visionaries involved in the project.

More than a construction milestone, the groundbreaking is a promise to the community and to future students: that this institution will stand as a symbol of growth, opportunity, and excellence.

As the foundation stone was set in place, it signified the beginning of a journey that is expected to shape the future of Papua’s workforce for years to come.
