Housekeeping Staff

Facility Staffing

Hotel-Quality Standards, On Site

PT. Pengembangan Jaya Papua (PJP) is a trusted provider of housekeeping labor supply services. With expertise across strategic, tactical, and operational aspects, we deliver quality cleanliness solutions tailored to each client's needs.

We understand the importance of creating a safe, hygienic environment that contributes to the overall health and satisfaction of everyone using a facility. Our housekeeping teams keep spaces clean and well-maintained, leaving a positive impression on every visitor.

Beyond appearance, a clean workplace has a real impact on productivity and performance — reducing the risk of damage, errors, and accidents. We recognise the value of a clean workplace in promoting focus, minimising disruption, and improving overall team efficiency.

A clean environment also helps reduce absenteeism, since employees are less likely to fall ill in a well-maintained workplace. Our housekeeping labor supply services prioritise cleanliness and hygiene while supporting our clients' organisational goals and reputation.

We recruit, train, and manage every housekeeping team we place, ensuring consistent standards of presentation and hygiene across camps, offices, and residential facilities throughout Papua.

  • Trained housekeeping staff for camps, offices, and residential facilities
  • Strategic, tactical, and operational cleanliness management
  • Reduced risk of damage, errors, and workplace accidents
  • Lower absenteeism through well-maintained environments
  • Hotel-quality presentation standards, consistently maintained
  • Full recruitment, training, and on-site supervision by PJP
