In-Flight Catering

Aviation Catering

Audited Meal Production For Airline Partners

PT. Pengembangan Jaya Papua (PJP) delivers outstanding in-flight catering to airlines serving Timika, Papua, including Airfast Indonesia, Garuda Indonesia, and Sriwijaya Air. Our expertise in preparing high-quality airline meals has made us a trusted partner in the aviation industry.

Our commitment to food safety and quality shows in every stage of production. We follow strict hygiene practices to ensure that all meals prepared and served on board meet the highest standards of excellence and safety.

Our experienced chefs and dedicated staff are known for meticulous meal preparation using fresh ingredients. Passengers on Airfast Indonesia, Garuda Indonesia, and Sriwijaya Air flights can expect a varied menu of delicious, nutritious options.

The professionalism and competence of our staff stand out, demonstrating expert food handling and cleanliness that leaves a positive impression with every airline partner we serve.

As a leading in-flight catering provider, we serve airlines operating in and out of Timika's aviation sector, allowing us to focus our specialisation on high-quality meals that support passenger satisfaction and well-being throughout every flight.

  • On-schedule meal production audited to airline standards
  • Trusted by Airfast Indonesia, Garuda Indonesia, and Sriwijaya Air
  • Strict hygiene and food-handling protocols for on-board service
  • Varied, nutritious menus prepared from fresh ingredients
  • Experienced aviation catering chefs and dedicated staff
  • Reliable service in and out of remote Papua airstrips
