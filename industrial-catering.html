Industrial Catering

Food Production & Catering

Buffet-Style Catering Built For Industrial Sites

PT. Pengembangan Jaya Papua (PJP) is the leading industrial catering provider in Timika, Papua, serving local companies in the corporate, mining, and construction sectors. We are a trusted catering partner for major organisations including Freeport, Petrosea, Leighton, Trakindo, and PanganSari.

We specialise in buffet-style catering designed to support productivity and well-being in demanding work environments. Our experienced chefs and dedicated staff prepare a diverse range of buffet menus from the freshest ingredients, tailored to the tastes of our corporate clients.

Food safety and quality come first. We follow strict cleanliness standards and robust food-handling practices to guarantee the highest level of safety for every meal we serve.

As a catering partner across Timika's industrial sector, we serve mining, construction, and heavy-equipment operations, focusing on high-quality food that keeps the workforce well-nourished, energised, and focused throughout demanding shifts.

Our professional staff manage every culinary detail so our clients can focus on their core operations without disruption — consistently delivering the delicious, nutritious meals that have earned the trust of clients across Timika.

  • Buffet-style service for camps, mess halls and job sites of any size
  • Experienced chefs and trained catering staff on every shift
  • Strict food safety, hygiene and halal standards
  • Trusted by Freeport, Petrosea, Leighton, Trakindo and PanganSari
  • Menus built from fresh, locally sourced ingredients
  • Full-scale production capacity for thousands of meals per day
