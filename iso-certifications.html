ISO Certifications

📅 2025🏷️ Quality

Since its founding in 2005, PT. Pengembangan Jaya Papua has grown into a workforce of 968 people, delivering catering and manpower services across Papua and preparing as many as 12,000 meals a day for the industrial sites and communities it serves. Operating at that scale, day after day, calls for food safety and quality systems that hold up under pressure — which is why PJP holds five formal certifications covering quality, food safety, occupational health & safety, environmental management, and halal compliance.

ISO 9001:2018

Quality Management System — a standard framework ensuring the organization consistently meets customer requirements across every catering and manpower operation.

ISO 22000:2018

Food Safety Management System — covers the essential steps in securing the food chain, from sourcing through to the final meal served.

ISO 45001:2018

Occupational Health & Safety Management System — manages the risks and opportunities that come with workplace health and safety.

ISO 14001:2015

Environmental Management System — the standard requirements for how the company manages its environmental responsibilities.

Halal Certificate

Issued by BPJPH (Badan Penyelenggara Jaminan Produk Halal), Indonesia's Halal Product Assurance Agency — formal recognition of the halal status of PJP's food products.

In practice, that means clear standard operating procedures for food handling, storage and preparation, alongside daily safety briefings, strict hygiene and sanitation protocols, cross-contamination prevention, and start-to-end monitoring of every production run. Water samples are tested periodically, and nutrition standards are measured, not assumed.

PJP's approach also extends to its people: regular staff audits and training, both internal and external inspections, technical and non-technical skills development, and a structured employee performance evaluation process. The goal is straightforward — every meal served, whether at a highland camp or for an in-flight catering client, should meet the same consistent standard of safety and quality.

This commitment to certified quality management isn't a one-time achievement but an ongoing discipline, reviewed and reinforced as PJP's operations continue to grow. It underpins the trust the company has built with clients across Papua's industrial, aviation, and events sectors over nearly two decades of service.
