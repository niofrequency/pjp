Job Fair at Horison Ultima Hotel

📅 November 7 – 9, 2023🏷️ Careers

In a dynamic showcase of opportunity and exclusivity, PT. Pengembangan Jaya Papua made a notable impact at the Exhibition & Job Fair at Horison Ultima Hotel in Papua Tengah, held from November 7 to 9, 2023.

What truly set the company apart was its unwavering commitment to diversity and local talent. PT. Pengembangan Jaya Papua proudly announced its goal of hiring 100% Papuans, representing a rich tapestry of the region with 60% from the 7 major tribes and 40% from other Papuan communities.

This emphasis on indigenous hiring reflected the company's dedication to fostering a workforce that mirrors the cultural and ethnic diversity of Papua, reinforcing its role not just as an employer but as a catalyst for empowerment and unity within the community. The job fair exclusively targeted Papuan talent, and the innovative barcode system garnered an overwhelming response, with 732 individuals registering their interest.

As the event concluded, PT. Pengembangan Jaya Papua emerged not only as a career destination but as a champion of Papuan talent and identity.
