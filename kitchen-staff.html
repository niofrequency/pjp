Kitchen Staff

Culinary Staffing

Trained Hands, Trusted Kitchens

PT. Pengembangan Jaya Papua (PJP) supplies and manages trained kitchen staff for catering operations across Timika, Papua — from head chefs to line cooks and kitchen hands. Our staffing expertise supports the same corporate clients we cater for directly, including Freeport, Petrosea, Leighton, Trakindo, and PanganSari.

Every kitchen team we place is built around buffet-style, high-volume production, designed to keep productivity and well-being high in demanding industrial and remote-camp environments. Our chefs and kitchen staff are experienced in preparing diverse menus from fresh ingredients at scale, and are trained to the standards our corporate clients expect.

Food safety and quality are non-negotiable. Every kitchen staff member we place follows strict cleanliness standards and robust food-handling practices, so clients can be confident in the safety of every meal their kitchen produces.

Our kitchen staff serve mining, construction, and heavy-equipment operations across Timika's industrial sector, contributing directly to workforce well-being through consistently high-quality food production.

We take pride in our professional kitchen teams, who work to exceed expectations and deliver a seamless catering experience — freeing clients to focus on their core operations rather than day-to-day kitchen management.

Consistency is central to how we staff and manage every kitchen. Our teams have built a reputation across Timika, Papua for reliability, discipline, and consistently excellent food production.

  • Head chefs, line cooks and kitchen hands, placed and managed end-to-end
  • Trained for high-volume, buffet-style production
  • Strict food safety, hygiene and halal standards on every shift
  • Experience serving Freeport, Petrosea, Leighton, Trakindo and PanganSari
  • Ongoing supervision and performance management by PJP
  • Scalable staffing for camps, events and aviation catering kitchens
