Nurdin's Farewell

📅 February 10, 2024🏷️ Community
Celebrating A Colleague

A Heartfelt Farewell to Nurdin: A Celebration of Dedication and Friendship

As the sun set on a remarkable chapter in the history of PT. Pengembangan Jaya Papua, the team bid a fond farewell to esteemed colleague Nurdin, who dedicated countless years to the company. The occasion was nothing short of extraordinary, as staff celebrated Nurdin's retirement with a unique and touching farewell gesture.

To commemorate Nurdin's years of service and friendship, PJP's creative team crafted a farewell lyric video for the song "Sampai Jumpa." Each member of the diverse staff held up signs featuring heartfelt lyrics, creating a touching visual representation of the shared memories and experiences built with Nurdin over the years. The video was a labor of love, reflecting the appreciation and respect held for the departing colleague.

The grand celebration continued on a Saturday, where Nurdin, surrounded by colleagues, friends, and well-wishers, had the opportunity to watch the beautiful lyric video. Emotions ran high as Nurdin watched the journey of his career unfold through the lens of the heartfelt song.

The company spared no effort in making the celebration memorable. A talented chef prepared a special menu that delighted the taste buds of all attendees. From savory dishes that pleased the palate to the refreshing sweetness of es buah, the culinary experience mirrored the richness of the moments shared with Nurdin.

In recognition of Nurdin's dedicated service, a certificate of retirement was presented to him, acknowledging his years of hard work, commitment, and valuable contributions. A thoughtful gift bag was also given as a token of gratitude, expressing the team's deepest appreciation for Nurdin's unwavering dedication.

The highlight of the evening was a heartwarming gesture from Nurdin's colleagues. In a touching display of camaraderie, all the co-workers gathered in a line to personally hug Nurdin and wish him well on his journey into retirement. Each embrace was filled with genuine warmth, laughter, and the shared memories that have become an integral part of the team's collective story.

In bidding farewell to Nurdin, PT. Pengembangan Jaya Papua reflects on the immeasurable impact he has had on the workplace, leaving an indelible mark on the hearts of everyone at the company. The team wishes Nurdin a retirement filled with joy, relaxation, and new adventures — may the next chapter of his life be as rewarding and fulfilling as the legacy he leaves behind. Sampai Jumpa, Nurdin!
